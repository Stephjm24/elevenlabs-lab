# Voice Design Zoo: incident log

Everything that went wrong, surprised us or cost more than it should, in the order it happened (Fri 20 Nov 2026, UK time). This feeds the "Where it broke" section of the README.

| # | Time | What happened | Cause | What we did / lesson |
|---|------|---------------|-------|----------------------|
| 1 | 16:48 | Claude's workspace couldn't download any preview mp3 (proxy 403). | The cloud workspace's network allowlist blocks `elevenlabsusercontent.com` and `storage.googleapis.com`. | Moved previews into an ElevenLabs flow with `creative_attach_reference_file` and measured them in the ElevenLabs sandbox instead. Fix for later: allow those domains in claude.ai Settings → Capabilities. |
| 2 | 16:52 | The voice-design design call for the robot butler was stopped by Steph. | Too much was being generated before anything had been heard. | Switched to a gated process: batches of 3, one step at a time, with Steph's OK between steps. Roster cut from 24 to 9 characters + 3 push cases. |
| 3 | 16:54 | Steph heard nothing from the voice-design players in the chat, the direct links or the flow nodes. | Steph's work laptop runs a managed Chrome (Symantec Endpoint Protection, organisation policy) that blocks `elevenlabsusercontent.com`. My Voices still played because saved-voice previews come from `storage.googleapis.com`. | Confirmed on a phone (plays fine). Listening steps moved to personal devices. Some saved voices (ghost, town crier) serve previews from `api.us.elevenlabs.io` instead, which may also be blocked. |
| 4 | 17:25 | Flow canvas: nodes placed far apart; no way to set positions through the tools. | The connector's flow tools have no position parameter; ElevenLabs auto-places nodes (lanes ~700 px apart, Line→Speak ~900 px). New batches ignore nodes Steph has dragged and can land on top of them. | Steph drags lanes together by hand after each build. |
| 5 | 17:41 | The tree's speech from its saved voice ran 19.5 s vs 30.2 s for the design preview of the same line. | A saved voice keeps the *sound* of the design (timbre, age, accent) but not the *delivery* the description asked for (pace, pauses). The TTS node never sees the description. | Logged as a finding. Fix would be in the line (audio tags / punctuation), not the voice. |
| 6 | 21:16 | ~850 credits used that don't appear on the flow. | Unknown: possibly activity elsewhere in ElevenLabs, or delayed billing. | Logged; usage numbers also lag (designs show up minutes later), which makes per-step accounting fuzzy. |
| 7 | 21:35 | Batch 3's Speak nodes were run twice (Steph at 21:29, Claude at 21:35). 614 credits wasted. | Claude didn't check the nodes' generation history before running. | Rule: read a node's history (`creative_get_flow_node`) before running it. Steph's 21:29 takes are the approved ones. |
| 8 | 21:36 | Claude started batch 4 designs straight after batch 3's run; Steph stopped them. | Two steps in one go, against the agreed process. | Back to one step per OK. |
| 9 | 21:38 | Mismatch push case (pirate description + earnings-call line): voice design returned "An unexpected error occurred". | Transient server error: the identical retry at 21:45 succeeded. | Retried once with Steph's OK. Not a content refusal; the mismatch itself designs fine. |
| 10 | 22:07 | Both talk-node estimates failed: "Referenced content generation not found: WrfoMEtxF4bOO27tLcCB". | The Speak · Lighthouse Keeper node's *active take* pointer referenced a generation that no longer exists, even though its history shows one healthy take (65QmmuO4IuKKHDDvPa1s). The picture node was fine. | Pinned the real take as a static audio node (`creative_add_flow_asset_node`) and rewired both talk nodes to it. Lesson: pin inputs before wiring them into expensive nodes. |
| 11 | 22:20 | Sandbox output was cut off: printing measurements plus an encoded contact-sheet image lost the measurements; even 8.5k characters were truncated. | The sandbox returns only roughly the last ~6,000 characters of stdout. | Keep sandbox output short (numbers, not images); fetch images in ≤5,500-character pieces, or use a numeric tracker instead. |
| 12 | 22:20 | Video generations have no preview frames in `creative_get_generations` (images do). | Tool limitation. | Claude can't see video frames directly; measured framing numerically instead. |
| 13 | 22:50 | **Aurora barely animated the sourdough starter.** Over 20 s the frame stayed 95% identical to the still picture and the mouth area moved ~20× less than on the human; ~17,000 credits for a near-still image. | Aurora is a talking-*head* model; a face made of bubbles behind glass is apparently not recognised as a face. (ElevenLabs' own guide warns that faceless images give near-static, still-charged video for a related model.) | Non-human characters: use OmniHuman, or test a cheap 480p draft first. |
