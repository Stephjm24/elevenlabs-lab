An open notebook for experiments.

I use ElevenLabs to follow a question and see what happens. My background in psychology and neuroscience makes me curious about things like how we hear emotion in a voice. Sometimes I just want to invent a radio station for a town that doesn’t exist.

Voice Design ZooVoice designIdea

What I’d try

I’d give three GPS voices the same directions and change only the character prompt. I’d compare the three readings side by side, then save the prompts with the clips so someone else can hear what changed.

Telephone GameSpeech and translationIdea

What I’d listen for

I’d put one sentence through 20 rounds of transcription, translation and revoicing. I want to catch the first round where the words still sound plausible but the meaning has slipped.

Choose your own adventureVoice agentsIdea

What I’d build

I’d write a short story with a fork in the road and let the listener choose by speaking. A few scenes later, I’d ask the narrator about the road we took. Does its answer fit the story we actually heard?

Imaginary radio stationSound and storytellingIdea

Where I’d start

A local DJ, one station jingle and a weather report from a place I’ve made up. I’d like to see how much of a town you can picture from a minute of radio.
